How MPMC Queue Works

  • Four Indices: Producer head/tail manage production, consumer head/tail manage consumption
  • Tagged Pointers: Format "index:tag" prevents ABA problems by including version numbers
  • Atomic Reservation: CAS operations atomically reserve slots for exclusive access
  • Ordered Completion: Tail advancement ensures FIFO order despite parallel operations
  • Race-Free Design: No thread can interfere with another's reserved slot
