Hey, I'm Dhairya 👋

Software Development Engineer at Microsoft working on distributed systems and high-throughput infrastructure for Azure Storage. I build scalable, fault-tolerant systems that handle massive workloads—from optimizing blob storage garbage collection to implementing state machines for automatic recovery across data, control, and customer planes.

My path into technology began with an Economics degree from IIT Bombay, complemented by a minor in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research. This interdisciplinary background built a strong statistical foundation that proved invaluable when I discovered my passion for building software systems. What started as curiosity about applying mathematical models to real-world problems evolved into leading technical teams and architecting scalable solutions.

Early in my journey, I took on leadership roles that shaped my approach to technology. As Manager of the Web Team at the Student Alumni Relations Cell, I led a five-member team through complex technical challenges, from implementing OAuth 2.0 authentication systems to architecting solutions that served over 10,000 alumni. This experience taught me that great technology isn't just about elegant algorithms—it's about understanding user needs, managing technical debt, and building systems that teams can maintain and scale.

My technical foundation deepened through diverse experiences across domains. At WorldQuant, I worked on high-frequency trading systems where microseconds matter, implementing Cython-based interfaces that achieved 100x performance improvements. This exposure to low-latency systems, combined with my statistical background, gave me a deep appreciation for the mathematical elegance underlying efficient distributed systems. Whether optimizing portfolio algorithms or building fault-tolerant storage solutions, I've learned to approach complex technical problems with both analytical rigor and practical engineering insight.

Today at Microsoft, I focus on building robust distributed systems and high-throughput infrastructure for Azure Storage. My work spans everything from garbage collection processes for blob storage to implementing state machines for automatic recovery systems. The combination of my quantitative background, leadership experience, and hands-on engineering has equipped me to tackle the complex trade-offs inherent in large-scale systems—whether that's optimizing P75 latency for high-performance workloads or orchestrating components across multiple service planes.
